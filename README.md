# 🎄 战舰世界 · 圣诞补给箱模拟器

在线模拟《战舰世界》2026 年圣诞补给箱（Santa's Gift）的开箱结果：143 艘船的完整船池、真实掉率与保底机制，看看你的运气能开出哪些船。

**▶ 在线使用（简体中文）：** https://zhouxf53.github.io/wows-santa-simulator/
**▶ English version：** https://zhouxf53.github.io/wows-santa-simulator/en/

无需安装，打开网页就能开箱。纯单页应用，所有数据只在你自己的浏览器里运行，不上传任何服务器。

## 功能

- **三种补给箱**：圣诞礼物 / 圣诞超级礼物 / 圣诞特级礼物，掉率与保底（礼物 100 / 超级 15 / 特级 7）按 15.11 封闭测试开发博客数据实现
- **完整圣诞船池**：143 艘船，按稀有 / 中级 / 普通分组；某一组抽空后，概率会重新分配给剩余组
- **金箱与战火锻造箱**：开出超级/特级箱有约 10% 概率升级为金色礼物箱；集齐全部 7 艘金色船后转为战火锻造高级补给箱（50% 出新泽西 BF，第 3 个保底必出）
- **同步你的船池**，已拥有的船不会重复开出：
  - 国际服：页面内 WG 登录，按确切 vehicle ID 匹配（B / 金色等不同版本分别计算）
  - 国服：点「登录 · 国服（教程）」，按教程把书签拖到书签栏，在军械库页面点一下即自动同步（仅电脑浏览器）；也可粘贴 ID 列表导入
  - 也可以手动勾选，或按船名搜索
- **批量开箱**：一次开 1–9,999 个，逐箱列出开到的船；统计区汇总出船率并与理论概率对比，给出带金边的「欧皇指数」徽章
- **稀有度庆祝**：开到稀有船有金色横幅 + 五彩纸屑，开到新泽西 BF 有专属烟花
- **分享战报**：一键生成开箱战报图（只有日期，不含昵称和 ID）
- **双语界面**：简体中文默认，可切换 English；船名使用社区通用译名，附英文小字对照

# 🎄 World of Warships — Santa's Gift Container Simulator

Simulate opening 2026 Santa's Gift containers in your browser: the full 143-ship pool, the real drop rates and pity counters — see which ships your luck brings.

**▶ Play (English)：** https://zhouxf53.github.io/wows-santa-simulator/en/
**▶ 简体中文版：** https://zhouxf53.github.io/wows-santa-simulator/

A single-page app. Everything runs locally in your browser; nothing is uploaded anywhere.

## Features

- **All three containers** — Santa's Gift / Mega Gift / Ultra Gift — with drop rates and pity counters (100 / 15 / 7) from the 15.11 closed-test dev blog
- **Full 143-ship Santa pool** in Rare / Mid / Common groups; when a group is exhausted, its chance redistributes to the groups still available
- **Golden containers & the War-Forged container**: Mega/Ultra opens have a ~10% chance to upgrade to a Golden Gift container; once you own all 7 Golden ships it converts to the War-Forged container (50% New Jersey BF, guaranteed by the 3rd)
- **Sync your own port** so owned ships never drop twice:
  - International servers: sign in with Wargaming on the page; ships are matched by exact vehicle ID (B / Golden variants counted separately)
  - CN server: use the "CN login (tutorial)" button — drag a bookmarklet to your bookmarks bar and click it on the Armory page to sync (desktop browsers only), or paste a list of ship IDs
  - Or just tick ships off manually, with name search
- **Bulk opening**: open 1–9,999 containers at once with a roll-by-roll list of every ship won, a stats panel comparing your actual drop rates against theory, and a gold-bordered luck-index badge
- **Rarity celebrations**: gold banner + confetti for Rare ships, dedicated fireworks for New Jersey BF
- **Share card**: generate a result image with the date only — no nickname, no account ID
- **Bilingual UI**: Simplified Chinese by default with an English toggle; ship names use the standard community translations with English subtitles

## Notes

- Drop rates come from the 15.11 closed-test dev blog and may change before release.
- Prices shown are last year's (including store bundle discounts) — official 15.11 prices are not published yet.
- Fan-made tool; not affiliated with Wargaming.
